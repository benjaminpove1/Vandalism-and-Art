# Vandalism-and-Art

🌍 Fighting vandalism and pollution through photocatalytic paint and urban art.

## 📌 About the Project
Vandalism-and-Art is a project that aims to turn vandalism into an opportunity for art. We use photocatalytic paint that absorbs pollution and stays clean, so people can create positive urban murals instead of damaging public spaces.

Our mission is for Ecuador to paint with purpose: fewer dirty walls and more color, identity, and care for the city.

## ⚠️ The Problem
Vandalism leaves walls, bridges, and public spaces deteriorated. At the same time, air and surface pollution worsens the image of cities and generates constant cleaning costs.

This leads to:
- Abandoned and neglected spaces.
- High costs from repeated cleaning.
- Less sense of safety and belonging.
- Lost opportunities for positive art and expression.

## 💡 Our Solutions
Vandalism-and-Art offers a practical way to fight vandalism:

1. **Photocatalytic paint**: Absorbs pollutants with sunlight and cleans itself.
2. **Art instead of vandalism**: Turns damaged walls into canvases for positive murals.
3. **Community participation**: Invites artists, students, and neighbors to paint with purpose.
4. **Less pollution**: Improves air quality and surface cleanliness in cities.
5. **Education and awareness**: Shows that painting can care for the city instead of harming it.

## 🎯 Goals
- Reduce vandalism by promoting positive urban art.
- Decrease pollution on surfaces and in nearby air.
- Give real tools to artists and communities.
- Build a sense of belonging and care for public spaces.
- Create cleaner and more colorful cities in Ecuador.

## 🌱 Expected Impact
Vandalism-and-Art aims for people to stop seeing vandalism as the only way to express themselves on walls. By offering a paint that cleans itself and lasts, it becomes easier to create art that improves the city instead of degrading it.

A wall painted with purpose can change the perception of a neighborhood and reduce the need for constant cleaning.
